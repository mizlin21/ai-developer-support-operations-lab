# What This Project Is

The AI Developer Support Operations Lab is a simulated enterprise support operations system designed to demonstrate how enterprise support teams can combine deterministic workflows, operational evidence, escalation systems, analytics, and governance-aware AI assistance.

The project demonstrates:

- AI-assisted developer support workflows
- evidence-based troubleshooting
- escalation workflows
- operational analytics
- governance and human review controls
